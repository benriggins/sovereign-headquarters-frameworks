# Issue 01 — Research

**Anchor video:** "The 3-Level Reward System That Ended My Burnout" (Benjamin Riggins, 2026-09-17). Full transcript and audio: https://sovereignheadquarters.com/podcast/video-01

## Anchor video summary

Benjamin Riggins was capped at about four hours of focused work a day. More rest left him rested and then burnt out again at the same point. He built a 3-Level Reward System, starting from Stephen Covey's "begin with the end in mind": a 25-year North Star (Level 3), milestones six months to five years out that feed it (Level 2), and micro rewards through the day after focused blocks of 30, 60 or 90 minutes (Level 1, his own protocol). Applying it took him from four hours of work a day to ten. That is his own result, not a promised one.

## The issue built on it

The 3-Level Reward System pays every hour of work three times: a 25-year North Star gives it meaning, milestones six months to five years out make progress visible, and micro rewards make today worth starting.

Thesis, as the evidence supports it: rest restores you; it doesn't redesign the work you return to. The issue extends the video with the editor's research files and keeps only claims checked against primary sources; claims with no findable primary source were dropped.
