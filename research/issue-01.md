**Anchor video:** [The 3-Level Reward System That Ended My Burnout](https://www.youtube.com/@BenjaminRiggins)

## Anchor video summary

Benjamin spent months capped at 4 hours of focused work a day before burning out — standard
advice to just "rest more" made it worse, since resting without a system just gave him a cleaner
slate to burn out again. He introduces the 3-Level Reward System he found through Stephen
Covey's North Star principle:

- **Level 3 — North Star.** A 25-year purpose bigger than yourself, everything else points back
  to it. His own is the Sovereign Association — a group uniting Christian entrepreneurs and
  reshaping how the world sees business.
- **Level 2 — Milestones and Goals.** 6-month to 5-year checkpoints that feed the North Star
  (his book, a monthly "AI for Christians at Work" roundtable).
- **Level 1 — Micro Rewards.** Daily rewards every 30–90 minutes that use dopamine's
  anticipation mechanism — not the reward itself — to sustain focus without burning out.

Applying the full system took him from 4 hours of daily work to 10 — a 250% increase — with the
effect compounding over time.
