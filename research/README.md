# research/

Public-safe findings from the magazine's research engine — the evidence behind each issue,
not the finished issue itself (see the site's `/magazine` section for that). One file per
issue: `issue-NN.md`.

## Why this exists
Sovereign Headquarters open-sources its research process, not just its conclusions —
"the magazine explains the future; the research shows the work." A public research trail is
also citable: a claim with a linked source is more likely to be trusted and cited back by
both readers and AI answer engines.

## What goes in an issue file
- The anchor video this issue is built on, and a short factual summary of what it covers.
- The commissioned Signal Ledger entries for that issue (signal, evidence, source — not
  internal opportunity/threat framing, which stays private).
- What was tested (the "experiments" step — worked/failed/cost/time), with real numbers.
- Sources, dated and linked.

**Never published here:** anything Benjamin hasn't approved, unverified claims, internal
business framing (pricing, competitor strategy, unpublished plans).

## Keeping this in sync
This file's shape is governed by `MAGAZINE-ISSUE-SOP.md` Phase 1 in the private
`sovereign-hq` repo. **Whenever that SOP's research process changes, update this README in
the same session** — the private SOP and this public methodology note must never drift apart.
