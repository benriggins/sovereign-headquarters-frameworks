# The 3-Level Reward System

*A named framework from The Sovereign Atlas, Issue 01 · Benjamin Riggins · Sovereign Headquarters*

## Definition

The 3-Level Reward System pays every hour of work three times: a 25-year North Star gives it meaning, milestones six months to five years out make progress visible, and micro rewards make today worth starting.

## The levels

| Level | Horizon | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| Foundation: God | Always | Who holds my life? |
| Level 3: North Star | About 25 years | Why? |
| Level 2: Milestones | 6 months to 5 years | What next? |
| Level 1: Micro rewards | The next 30 to 90 minutes | What now? |

Build it from the top down: Level 2 has to feed Level 3, and Level 1 has to feed Level 2.

## Boundaries

His move from four to ten hours of work a day is his own result. The 30/60/90-minute rhythm is his protocol, not a proven interval. The system is not a treatment for burnout.
