## What's open

**The full text of every article.** Nothing sits behind a paywall or a signup. Read it, share it and quote it, as long as you credit the source.

**The framework.** This is the definition we'd like you to use:

> **The 3-Level Reward System** is a way of structuring work around rewards at three time scales: a North Star you are working toward over 25 years (Level 3), milestones six months to five years out that feed it (Level 2), and micro rewards that get you through today (Level 1).

**The sources.** Every research claim in this issue links to its primary source. Here they are in one place, grouped by the question they answer. The full citations sit at the foot of this page.

- **Does rest work?** Grant, Buchanan and Shockley's [2025 vacation meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1037/apl0001262); de Bloom and colleagues' [2009 meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1539/joh.k8004) on fade-out after vacation; the WHO's [ICD-11 description of burnout](https://www.who.int/news/item/28-05-2019-burn-out-an-occupational-phenomenon-international-classification-of-diseases).
- **Why does purpose matter?** Sheldon and Elliot's [self-concordance model](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.76.3.482); Ryan and Deci's [self-determination theory](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.55.1.68) and its [application to work](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-orgpsych-032516-113108).
- **Why do milestones work?** Locke and Latham's [goal-setting theory](https://doi.org/10.1037/0003-066X.57.9.705); Bandura's [self-efficacy](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-295X.84.2.191); Bandura and Schunk on [proximal goals](https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.41.3.586); Byars-Winston and colleagues' [sources-of-self-efficacy meta-analysis](https://doi.org/10.1037/cou0000219); Wright's [1936 learning curve](https://doi.org/10.2514/8.155).
- **Why does today's block work?** Amabile and Kramer's [“The Power of Small Wins”](https://hbr.org/2011/05/the-power-of-small-wins) and [HBS's summary](https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/how-small-wins-unleash-creativity); Gollwitzer and Sheeran on [implementation intentions](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0065-2601(06)38002-1); Lally and colleagues on [habit formation](https://doi.org/10.1002/ejsp.674).
- **What about rewards and dopamine?** Schott and colleagues on [reward anticipation](https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.2058-08.2008); Shenhav, Botvinick and Cohen's [expected value of control](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neuron.2013.07.007); Deci, Koestner and Ryan's [128-study warning](https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.125.6.627); Albulescu and colleagues on [micro-breaks](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0272460).
- **The anchor:** Benjamin's [video and full transcript](https://sovereignheadquarters.com/podcast/video-01).

Scripture quotations come from the ESV and NIV, each named and linked where it appears.

What's missing matters too. Our research notes contained statistics we could not trace to a primary source. They aren't softened here. They're gone.
